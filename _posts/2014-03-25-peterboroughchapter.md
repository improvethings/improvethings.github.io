## Information

We are meeting on a biweekly basis, typically at The Spill Cafe, at 7pm on Monday's. All open minded critical thinkers are welcome!

Our meetings have a simple agenda, all we do is meet together over drinks to discuss ideas, and see how we can help each other make them happen! It can be any type of idea; how to make the city of Peterborough more awesome, a business idea, an idea how how we can make ourselves better, or a product idea... no limit!

Check back regularly or join the: [Ideas - Peterborough Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/pages/Ideas-Peterborough/366318543470528) or follow on Twitter [@ideasptbo](https://twitter.com/ideasptbo)

There are a few individuals who are carrying the administrative and communication load at present. Details can be found on the [Administrivia Notes page](http://archive.ideasmeetings.org/wiki/Peterborough_AdminNotes).

## Announcements
