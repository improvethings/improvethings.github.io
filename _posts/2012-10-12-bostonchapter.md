## Information

The Ideas Meetings in Boston started in April 2012. Since then we have been meeting once each month near Kendall Square, on a week night from 7 to 9. All open minded critical thinkers are welcome!

Currently there is no mailing list for Boston. In the meantime, join our [Facebook Page](http://www.facebook.com/BostonIdeas).
