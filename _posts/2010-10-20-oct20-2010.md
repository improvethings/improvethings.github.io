Several cities globally have expressed an interest in having an ideas meeting of their own. Oct 20th will be a hack night where we make this process easy for them.

* Needed done before this evening, agree on one of the [domainnames](http://archive.ideasmeetings.org/wiki/domainnames) and register it.
* Move this wiki over to the new domain

## Hack night

Create plan of attack, which will include:
* Defining what it takes for other cities to create and regularly host an ideas meeting
* Make this wiki more pretty (CSS, graphic design)
* Design a brand logo/image/colour scheme
