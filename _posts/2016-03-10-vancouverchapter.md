## Information

Welcome to the Ideas - Vancouver wiki. All open minded critical thinkers are welcome! As we often forget to update this page, you're always best to also check the [Facebook group](https://www.facebook.com/ideasvan) for the latest. If you're on meetup.com, we publish our events in the [Vancouver Pixel Crafters](http://www.meetup.com/Vancouver-Tech-Co-Founders/) group. If you're new to Vancouver, like to make things happen, or want to meet other creative doers in the community, come join us. 

## Current Projects
