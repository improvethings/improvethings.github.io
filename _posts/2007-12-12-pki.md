The idea of this project is to use the functionality of openssl on a website, creating certificates for free for people.

[CA Cert](http://www.cacert.org/) has done something similar. They are interesting to follow, as they were incorporated into the [Firefox](http://hackepedia.org/?title=Firefox) web browser, but were then removed shortly afterwards.
