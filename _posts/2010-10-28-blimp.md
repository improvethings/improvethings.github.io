We have started discussion on making a hydrogen powered blimp. 

* Remote controlled
* Can handle a webcam
* autonomous 
* Ideally it will generate hydrogen on the fly, refilling the balloon and using the hydrogen to control it (jet powered by hydrogen sounds like it has potential)
* GPSr are on board, potentially also APRS (ham radio transmitting GPS data; one purpose could be to try and keep the blimp in a certain area)
* [open source helicopter](http://autopilot.sourceforge.net/index.html)
* check with Transport Canada about their rules around air space usage
* determine virtual reality engine to use

## Virtual Reality

We would like to remotely control the blimp, while viewing from the camera onboard the blimp. This provides us the opportunity as well to create a virtual reality.
