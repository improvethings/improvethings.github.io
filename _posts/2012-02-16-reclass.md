Interested in going from 0-90 in software? ([Reverse Engineering](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reverse_engineering) is taking software apart and understanding it) 
We're going to run a community based reverse engineering class. Everyone is welcome, no matter what level of experience you have. We're starting at 0, but will likely advance quite rapidly together. 

* Guy R to draft the initial syllabus
  * Will include introduction to computer parts, architecture and assembly language
* Planning meeting at the next ideas meeting after it's posted
  * Introductions, round table of where each person is at and what they can contribute, what they're hoping to get out of the class
  * Edit syllabus as a group until consensus 
  * Picking a regular location/date and time
* Hack/Make the world better
