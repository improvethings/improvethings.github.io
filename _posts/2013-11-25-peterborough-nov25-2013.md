RELP (a working title from Relationship, obvs)
* a database and set of web based interfaces documenting community happenings
* goal is to capture in one place all the great things that have gone on and are going on
* this will hopefully show that individual things are not the whole story by a long shot - relationships exist and matter! community is strong.
* already have an informal pool of sample data waiting for digitizing and have had preliminary discussions with a variety of organizations
* need some technically skilled individuals, some data processing help, some funding/resources
* currently studying [ThinkMap](http://thinkmap.com) as one interface
* if engaged/interested contact Mark @perspicuity1
