## Obligatory Conversation About Advice For Start-Ups
* Start in a market with an easier barrier to entry then expand to your originally desired market.
  * SRRN Games started with iOS games and are now trying to move into other platforms.
  * Netflix started with DVDs by mail but had always planned to focus on streaming.
* Narrow your focus within a market.
  * travel website just focused on Costa Rica at first
  * Facebook, just students initially

## An Alternate Kind of Classical Theater/Music Venue
* Shakespeare used to be performed to a loud, rowdy audience
  * Boring to just sit respectfully with your hands folded in your lap and not talking to anyone
  * Let's try to attract a younger audience, a new generation of theater-goers
  * Maybe the actors (and other kinds of performers) are creating this atmosphere as a response to an otherwise casual and irreverent culture?
* Examples of this already happening:
  * July 4th @ Hatch Shell
  * Shakespeare on the Common (but people are kinda quiet)
  * Hollywood Bowl
  * Sleep No More
  * Renaissance Faires
  * Medieval Times
  * historic villages
  * comedy shows / improv.
    * the interaction is with the performer, not to other audience members
    * only when called upon by the performers
  * vaudeville
  * Rocky Horror Picture Show
  * The Donkey Show
* Strategies:
  * musicals
  * shorter acts
  * lights on
  * dinner theater
* Clearly there are cultural differences in expectations

## Tech Tool to Help Political Independents
* Reach more people to make running for office a more realistic possibility.
* Conventional social networks are too polluted.
* Target college students
  * who might still be on the fence or are more open-minded
  * stubborn affiliation hasn't taken root yet
  * Facebook started with college students and grew into a more general user base as those students graduated
  * especially those at Harvard, etc.
    * when they enter the US Government, they will bring these ideas and use of this tool with them
* Media cannot be unbiased.
  * American news outlets are just part of the entertainment industry.
  * Artists have a responsibility to educate the public instead of commercializing and dumbing it down.
  * Can it be like Wikipedia but in a very fast way?
    * Independent, neutral moderators
      * Do they exist?
      * Let people vote to establish a \"neutrality meter\" for each article or editor.
    * citation needed, footnotes
      * then we wouldn't have the news
      * we'd have to report less news
    * just report the facts, no editorials
      * how to verify disputed facts (like, how many people attended a rally?)
      * trying to calculate would be more analysis and not just fact-reporting, could be biased
* We need a disruptive political movement.
* How to get independent things mainstream exposure?
* We need better public education
  * so citizens can think critically about issues
  * instead of just consuming what the media is force-feeding them.
