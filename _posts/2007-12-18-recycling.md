Why is recycling not cost effective in Canada?

This is a research project. It has been suggested that Halifax is the only city in Canada where the recycling program is financially sustainable. We will look into this, and if it's true, why is it true?
Also, what are other countries doing?

If you can contact people willing top provide us feedback on recycling, or do your own research, we can use this page to post our results. Feel free to discuss this on [Talk:Recycling](http://archive.ideasmeetings.org/wiki/Talk_Recycling).
