### New Ideas and Notes from meeting

* Social Landscape Map
  * to eliminate the silos of organizations in the community
  * commercial software for social mapping: Mark has been working up a speech/presentation for RELP (see below)
  * need a map of the central core of Ptbo initiatives: who does what, what are the services they offer
  * some of the larger orgs are maybe abusing some of their privileges while the smaller orgs are stifled because they're all going after the same money
  * hell hath no fury like a charity protecting itself
  * are there too many vested interests? charity posts used as rewards for political vehicles?
  * a curious, socially charged citizen of the community maybe should document it, when one sees people in poverty and then the questionable aspect is happening. 
  * question: is the point that the money doesn't come out the bottom end of the funnel and actually serve the people it was meant to? yes.
  * status quo is if you don't know somebody, forget it
  * can do it as a curious person, going in with an agenda
  * with funding, how many salaries does that cover? discussion of the 'poverty industry'
  * there are 23 employment agencies in Ptbo, all publicly funded
  * Ptbo has the highest number of not-for-profit orgs in Canada
  * put somebody in the hotseat when asked questions in a curiosity mission, questions really need to be asked with what's happening with poverty and homelessness in our community
  * you give money to United Way and feel good, and go away
  * log identification workshop mark updated
  * so many agencies developing jobs, but what jobs?
